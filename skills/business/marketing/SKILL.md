# Marketing

Use this skill to explain value to audiences and to build demand. Sales conversations and legal or compliance claims need other skills.

## Scope

**Owns:**
- How we describe the offer and what we say (positioning and messaging: the problem, what makes us different, and proof)
- Audience definition and channel strategy
- Content planning (docs, demos, videos, case studies, newsletters)
- Launch and release communication (aligned with product readiness)
- Brand voice guidelines for a cooperative and open-hardware context
- The story we tell the community (without replacing `open-source-community` governance)

**Does NOT own:**
- Final prices → `finance`, `business-development`
- Contract promises in ads → `legal-advice`
- CE (EU safety mark) or marketing claims about safety or conformity → `product-compliance`
- Managing individual sales deals → `sales`
- Technical accuracy of specs → engineering skills

## Core principles

- **Honest hardware marketing:** Never overstate certification, performance, or availability. Stock and wait times must match `supply-chain-management` reality.
- **Show the work:** Open hardware marketing works better with build logs, a clear bill of materials (BOM), and demos others can repeat.
- **Community is not the same as audience:** Contributors and customers overlap, but they need different messages. Coordinate with `open-source-community`.
- **Cooperative identity:** Stress shared ownership, sustainability, and products that work with others, when that is true.
- **Documentation is marketing:** Good install guides and assembly docs reduce support work and build trust.

## Key workflows

### Positioning statement (template)

For **[segment]** who **[need]**, **[venture]** is **[category]** that **[key benefit]**. Unlike **[alternative]**, we **[differentiator grounded in open hardware/cooperative values]**.

Check this with `business-development` evidence. Check technical claims with engineering.

### Launch checklist

- [ ] Messaging reviewed for regulatory claims (`product-compliance` if safety or CE is mentioned)
- [ ] Assets match the current hardware revision and software tag
- [ ] Channels chosen for the audience (makers, industry, academia)
- [ ] Support and docs links ready (`software-development`, assembly guides)
- [ ] Internal agreement on pricing and availability (`sales`, `finance`)
- [ ] Log entry per `log` skill

### Content types for open hardware

| Type | Purpose |
| --- | --- |
| Assembly / user guides | Fewer returns, and contributors can help |
| Comparison tables | Honest comparison with alternatives |
| Roadmap (high level) | Set expectations without promising too much |
| Contributor stories | Strengthen `open-source-community` |

## Terminology

- Ideal customer profile (ICP). Early, middle, and late buyer stages (TOFU / MOFU / BOFU). Call to action (CTA). Search engine optimization (SEO) and related discovery (GEO) basics.
- Open-source hardware (OSHW), open core (use carefully — legal boundaries → `legal-advice`)

## Practical constraints

- Do not publish CE marks or safety statements without `product-compliance` confirmation.
- Do not publish hardware photos until supply and the revision are stable.
- GNU General Public License (GPL) and other license duties affect how you share images of combined works. Ask `legal-advice` for edge cases.
