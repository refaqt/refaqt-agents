# Marketing

Use this skill to communicate value to audiences and build demand. Sales conversations and legal/compliance claims require other skills.

## Scope

**Owns:**
- Positioning and messaging (problem, differentiation, proof points)
- Audience definition and channel strategy
- Content planning (docs, demos, videos, case studies, newsletters)
- Launch and release communication (aligned with product readiness)
- Brand voice guidelines for cooperative/open-hardware context
- Community-facing narrative (without replacing `open-source-community` governance)

**Does NOT own:**
- Pricing authority → `finance`, `business-development`
- Contractual commitments in ads → `legal-advice`
- CE/marketing claims on safety or conformity → `product-compliance`
- Individual pipeline management → `sales`
- Technical accuracy of specs → engineering skills

## Core principles

- **Truthful hardware marketing:** Never overstate certification, performance, or availability; stock and lead times must match `supply-chain-management` reality.
- **Show the work:** Open hardware marketing benefits from build logs, BOM transparency, and reproducible demos.
- **Community ≠ audience:** Contributors and customers overlap but need different messages; coordinate with `open-source-community`.
- **Cooperative identity:** Emphasize shared ownership, sustainability, and interoperability where authentic.
- **Documentation is marketing:** Good install guides and assembly docs reduce support load and build trust.

## Key workflows

### Positioning statement (template)

For **[segment]** who **[need]**, **[venture]** is **[category]** that **[key benefit]**. Unlike **[alternative]**, we **[differentiator grounded in open hardware/cooperative values]**.

Validate with `business-development` evidence; verify technical claims with engineering.

### Launch checklist

- [ ] Messaging reviewed for regulatory claims (`product-compliance` if safety/CE mentioned)
- [ ] Assets match current hardware revision and software tag
- [ ] Channels chosen for segment (makers, industry, academia)
- [ ] Support and docs links ready (`software-development`, assembly guides)
- [ ] Internal alignment on pricing and availability (`sales`, `finance`)
- [ ] Log entry per `log` skill

### Content types for open hardware

| Type | Purpose |
| --- | --- |
| Assembly / user guides | Reduce returns, enable contributors |
| Comparison matrices | Honest vs. alternatives |
| Roadmap (high level) | Set expectations without over-promising |
| Contributor stories | Strengthen `open-source-community` |

## Practical constraints

- Do not publish CE marks or safety statements without `product-compliance` confirmation.
- Embargo hardware photos until supply and revision are stable.
- GPL and other license obligations affect how you distribute images of combined works — consult `legal-advice` for edge cases.
